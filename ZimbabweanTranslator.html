ZSL signs are always preferred. Every word is checked against the ZSL picture collection and scanned dictionary; alternate English labels, common grammatical variants, and words within multiword entries are matched too. If no ZSL sign is available, the corresponding ASL sign illustration is shown directly. ASL illustrations are labelled and are not ZSL signs; words absent from both dictionaries use fingerspelling.
